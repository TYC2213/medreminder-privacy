MedReminder（以下稱「本應用程式」）是協助使用者安排服藥提醒及記錄服藥情況的工具。本政策說明本應用程式處理哪些資料、處理目的、第三方服務與使用者選擇。

一、適用範圍與基本原則

本政策適用於 MedReminder 的 iOS 版本。本應用程式以在使用者裝置上處理服藥管理資訊為設計原則，不要求使用者建立開發者營運的雲端帳號。本政策不表示所有 SDK 或作業系統功能均完全離線運作。

二、本應用程式處理的資料

2.1 服藥管理與紀錄

使用者自行輸入或操作的資料可能包括藥品或藥包名稱、時間、頻率、週期、服藥狀態、實際服藥時間、歷史紀錄及提醒偏好。這些資料用於顯示清單、安排提醒、修改及查詢歷史紀錄。

2.2 藥品照片與相機

經使用者操作及必要授權，本應用程式可使用相機拍攝、裁切並顯示藥品或藥包照片，以協助辨識藥物。開發者不會為廣告定向主動將這些照片傳送給 Google AdMob。

2.3 位置與返家提醒

使用者啟用相關功能後，本應用程式可能存取使用者設定的住家位置，以及進出指定範圍的定位事件，用於返家服藥提醒。定位權限可在 iOS 設定中管理或撤銷。此功能的可靠性亦受裝置權限、背景執行及系統限制影響。開發者不會主動把使用者設定的住家位置傳給 AdMob 作為廣告定向資訊。

2.4 鬧鐘、通知與設定

本應用程式使用 iOS 通知及適用裝置的 AlarmKit 功能安排服藥提醒，並保存相關排程偏好及提醒操作結果。通知或鬧鐘功能可能需要系統授權。

三、本機儲存、備份及換機

服藥管理資料以本機優先方式處理；本應用程式目前不提供由開發者營運的服藥紀錄雲端同步帳號。使用者啟用 Apple 的 iCloud 備份、快速開始移轉或電腦備份時，符合 Apple 備份規則的 App 資料可能由 Apple 服務備份或轉移；備份範圍與還原結果取決於 iOS 設定及資料的實際存放位置。新裝置可能須重新授權定位、通知並重建提醒排程。

刪除 App 可能刪除裝置上的 App 資料；先前存在的裝置備份可能依 Apple 的機制保留。使用者應自行維護必要的備份。

四、第三方廣告服務與可能的網路資料處理

本應用程式整合 Google AdMob 廣告服務。Google 與其廣告合作夥伴可能依使用者所在地、裝置設定、同意狀態與適用法律，處理 IP 位址、裝置或廣告識別碼、裝置與 App 技術資訊、廣告曝光與互動資料，以及效能和診斷資料，用於廣告提供、成效衡量、安全防護及防詐欺；在允許的情況下也可能用於個人化廣告。

本應用程式不會主動把使用者輸入的服藥紀錄、藥品照片或使用者設定的住家位置提供給 AdMob 作為廣告定向資料。拒絕個人化廣告不必然代表所有廣告都不顯示；在適用條件下仍可能顯示非個人化廣告。

五、廣告同意與裝置權限

本應用程式會依適用地區、法律及技術要求提供必要的廣告同意管理。使用者可在相關同意介面管理選擇，也可在 iOS 設定中管理相機、定位、通知及適用的追蹤權限。撤銷權限可能影響依賴該權限的提醒功能。

六、付費功能與購買資料

本應用程式未來可能提供付費去廣告及其他功能；在功能正式上線後，相關購買預計透過 Apple App Store 內購服務執行。Apple 負責交易及付款資訊處理；本應用程式可能使用 Apple 提供的交易狀態驗證或恢復購買資格。這並不要求將服藥紀錄上傳至開發者伺服器。正式推出前會按實際功能更新本政策。

七、資料分享與跨境處理

開發者不以販售使用者服藥紀錄、藥品照片或住家位置作為商業模式。第三方平台（如 Apple 備份服務及 Google AdMob）可能依各自隱私政策、法律要求及使用者操作，在使用者所在地以外處理相關技術或服務資料。使用者主動透過系統分享功能提供的資料，由其選擇的接收服務依其規則處理。

八、資訊安全

本應用程式利用 iOS 的 App 隔離與權限機制保護本機資訊，並致力於維持合理的資料安全措施。但任何裝置或網路服務均無法保證絕對安全。使用者應妥善設定裝置解鎖方式並管理備份。

九、資料保存與刪除

本機資料一般依使用者操作及裝置儲存狀態保存；使用者可使用 App 中提供的功能修改或刪除紀錄，也可刪除 App。移除 App 不必然刪除先前建立的 iCloud 或其他系統備份。第三方廣告服務所處理的資訊，依其各自的保存及刪除規則管理。

十、使用者權利

使用者可依適用法律，請求查閱、更正、刪除個人資料或行使其他法定權利。對於儲存在裝置內的資料，使用者可透過 App 功能及裝置控制操作；對於 Apple 或 Google 獨立處理的資料，可能需要直接向相關服務商提出請求。

十一、未成年人

本應用程式並非專門面向兒童設計。未成年人應依適用法律及實際需要在法定代理人協助下使用。開發者不會主動利用服藥資訊進行針對未成年人的廣告定向。

十二、政策更新

本政策可能隨功能、法規或第三方服務變更而修訂，並於本頁更新日期。若涉及重大新資料用途，將在開始相關處理前依法律要求告知使用者並取得必要同意。更新本政策本身不會取代依法應取得的同意。

MedReminder (the “App”) helps users schedule medication reminders and record medication activity. This policy explains what information the App handles, why it is used, which third parties may process data, and the choices available to users.

1. Scope and principles

This policy applies to the iOS version of MedReminder. The App is designed to manage medication information primarily on the user's device and does not require an account hosted by the developer. This does not mean that all operating system functions or third party SDKs operate entirely offline.

2. Information handled by the App

2.1 Medication schedules and records

Information entered or generated through user actions may include medication or package names, scheduled times, frequency, duration, medication status, actual administration times, history, and reminder preferences. The App uses this information to present lists, schedule reminders, and allow users to review or edit records.

2.2 Medication photos and camera

When requested by the user and permitted by the operating system, the App may use the camera to capture, crop, store, and display photos of medications or medication packages. The developer does not intentionally transmit these photos to Google AdMob for advertising targeting.

2.3 Location and return home reminders

When enabled, the App may access a home location selected by the user and location events indicating entry into or departure from a defined area, solely to support return home medication reminders. Users may manage or revoke location permissions in iOS Settings. The reliability of this feature depends on permissions, background execution, and operating system conditions. The developer does not intentionally send a user's configured home location to AdMob for ad targeting.

2.4 Alarms, notifications, and settings

The App uses iOS notifications and, on supported devices, AlarmKit to schedule medication reminders and manage related preferences and user responses. These functions may require operating system permissions.

3. Local storage, backups, and device migration

Medication information is handled using a local first approach. The App does not currently offer a developer operated cloud account for synchronizing medication records. If a user enables Apple's iCloud Backup, Quick Start migration, or computer backups, App data eligible under Apple's backup rules may be backed up or migrated. The exact scope and success of restoration depend on iOS settings and actual storage locations. Permissions and native alarm schedules may need to be reestablished on a new device.

Removing the App may delete local App data, while earlier device backups may remain available under Apple's policies. Users should maintain backups as appropriate.

4. Third party advertising and network data

The App integrates Google AdMob. Depending on location, device settings, consent status, and applicable law, Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, technical device and App information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These details may be used for ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

The App does not intentionally provide user entered medication records, medication photos, or configured home locations to AdMob for ad targeting. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent the display of all advertising. Non personalized advertising may still be shown where permitted.

5. Advertising consent and device permissions

Where required by law or applicable technical rules, the App provides mechanisms to manage advertising consent. Users may also manage camera, location, notification, and applicable tracking permissions through iOS Settings. Withdrawing permission may affect features that rely on it.

6. Purchases and paid features

The App may offer ad removal or other paid features in the future. If introduced, purchases are expected to use Apple's App Store in app purchase system. Apple handles payments and related transaction details. The App may use Apple transaction status to verify or restore entitlements. This does not require uploading medication records to the developer's servers. This policy will be updated to reflect the actual implementation before release of such features.

7. Sharing and international processing

The developer does not operate a business model based on selling users' medication records, medication photos, or configured home locations. Third party platforms, including Apple backup services and Google AdMob, may process relevant service or technical information outside a user's country in accordance with their own policies, applicable law, and user choices. Information a user explicitly shares through operating system features is handled by the chosen recipient service under its terms.

8. Security

The App relies in part on iOS application isolation and permission controls to protect local information and seeks to maintain reasonable security practices. No device or online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect access to their devices and manage backups appropriately.

9. Retention and deletion

Local information generally remains subject to user actions and device storage conditions. Users may edit or delete records through available App features or remove the App. Removing the App may not delete existing iCloud or other device backups. Information independently processed by advertising providers is subject to their own retention and deletion practices.

10. User rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, erase, or otherwise control personal data. Locally stored information may be managed through App features or device controls. Requests regarding data independently processed by Apple or Google may need to be directed to those providers.

11. Children

The App is not specifically designed for children. Minors should use it with appropriate assistance from a parent or legal guardian where required by law or circumstances. The developer does not intentionally use medication information to target advertisements to children.

12. Policy updates

This policy may change as features, laws, or third party services evolve. The update date will be revised on this page. Where a material new data use requires advance notice or consent, the developer will provide it before that processing begins. Updating this page alone does not replace consent required by law.
